How the fund is built

Sleeve Weight Holds Rule

When it trades

  1. Every weekday at 07:00 UK the model re-scores everything on the previous day’s closing prices.
  2. It suggests a rebalance only when a holding enters or leaves, the market warning level changes, or a position drifts at least 3 points and a quarter of its size from target.
  3. After a rebalance it waits 10 trading days before the next one, unless market risk rises.
  4. Holdings get slack before they are sold: a stock stays until it drops out of the top 8, and a region until it drops out of the top 4.

This is a rules-based model, not regulated financial advice. Past and backtested returns do not guarantee future ones. Trading costs assumed: 0.10% per ETF trade, 0.25% for US and European stocks (includes Trading 212’s 0.15% currency fee), 0.5% stamp duty on UK share purchases.
