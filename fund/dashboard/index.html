When it trades

  1. Every weekday at 06:35 UK the model re-scores both pies on the previous day’s closing prices.
  2. Pie A suggests a rebalance when a holding enters or leaves, the market warning level changes, or a position drifts at least 3 points and a quarter of its size. It then waits 10 trading days unless market risk rises.
  3. Pie B trades less often because small caps swing more: a position must drift at least half its size, and it waits 20 trading days between rebalances.
  4. Money moves between the pies only when one reaches 45% or 55% of the fund.
  5. Pie B skips stocks with an agreed takeover, spotted by a price pinned near the offer and collapsed volatility.

This is a rules-based model, not regulated financial advice. Past and backtested returns do not guarantee future ones. Pie B can fall further than the index in a crash, and with 12 stocks a single company can move it by several points. Costs assumed: 0.10% per ETF trade, 0.25% for US and European stocks (includes Trading 212’s 0.15% currency fee), 0.5% stamp duty on UK share purchases, and an extra 0.15% spread on pie B trades.
