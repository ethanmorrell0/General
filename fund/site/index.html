    How it works
    PartWeightHoldsRule
    1. Every weekday the model re-scores both pies on the latest closing prices: at 22:35 UK after the US close, and again at 10:00 UK to catch anything missed. Sync now runs it straight away.
    2. It only suggests trades when they matter: a holding enters or leaves, market risk changes, or a slice drifts well off target. After a rebalance pie A waits 10 trading days and pie B 20.
    3. New cash goes to whichever pie and slices are furthest below target, so you rarely need to sell.
    4. Money moves between the pies only when one reaches 45% or 55% of the fund.

    A rules-based model, not regulated financial advice. Past and backtested returns don’t guarantee future ones. “Worth now” is an estimate from the model’s own prices and assumes you follow its trades; your Trading 212 balance is the real number.
