How it works

A scheduled Claude task fetches odds, fits the models and writes the priced board here. Refresh runs that task now; new prices land in about five minutes.

Fair prices come from Pinnacle and exchange odds with the margin removed, blended with a Dixon-Coles football model and points ratings for NFL, NBA and college sports. Edge = fair probability × bet365 odds − 1. On football match results, the chance of going two goals up without winning is added for bet365's 2-Up payout.

Type the price bet365 shows into a card to confirm the edge. Your prices, settings and bets are saved to your account and sync across devices.
